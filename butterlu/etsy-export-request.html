Data request

What we need from the Etsy shop

Etsy has no way to give someone else access to a shop — no staff logins, no invites. So these have to come from you. Every link below opens the right page directly once you're signed in.

Time needed ~15 minutes Items 5 Send to whoever asked for this

Files to download

  1. 01

    Currently for Sale Listings

    The most important one. If you only do one, do this.

    https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/download

    If the link doesn't work: Shop Manager→Settings→Options→Download Data tab→under Currently for Sale Listings, press Download CSV.

    What's in itTitle, description, price, currency, quantity, tags, materials, image URLs and SKU for every active listing. The tags are the part we most need — 13 per listing.

  2. 02

    Orders

    Shows what actually sells, and at what price.

    https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/download

    Same page as above. Scroll to Orders→pick a CSV Type, then Month and Year.

    TipLeave Month empty to get a whole year in one file. Last two full years is ideal. Order Items is the most useful CSV type if you have to pick one.

  3. 03

    Shop settings & reviews

    Quick, and the reviews tell us where orders go wrong.

    https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/download

    Same page→under Take your data with you→Download Your Shop Settings and Download Your Reviews.

Screens to photograph

  1. 04

    The search terms shoppers used

    Screenshots — no download exists

    Etsy gives you no way to export this. It's also the single most valuable thing on this list.

    https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/stats/referrers/etsysearch

    If the link doesn't work: Shop Manager→Stats→under How shoppers found you, choose Etsy search.

    What to captureSet the date range as far back as it will go, then screenshot the whole list of search terms. Scroll and keep shooting until you reach the end — the long tail matters as much as the top.

  2. 05

    Marketplace Insights

    Screenshots — 15 free searches per week

    Real Etsy search volume, straight from Etsy. Free, but capped — don't burn the 15 on guesses.

    https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/stats

    Shop Manager→Stats→Marketplace Insights→type a keyword.

    Search these five firstgodmother frame · godparent gift · baptism gift · personalized picture frame · grandma frame
    Screenshot each result. Results stay available for 7 days, and the weekly allowance resets.

Two quick questions while you're in there

  • Which shops are still live? Three names show up in the old product descriptions — ButterLu, BoutiqueButterLu and myfourlittlechicks. We need the files from each one that's still active, and we should retire any dead links still printed on the products.
  • Is there an export button anywhere on the Stats page? We couldn't find one in Etsy's documentation, so we're assuming screenshots. If you spot one, use it instead — it beats photographing screens.

Optional, and slower

A complete archive of everything Etsy holds, in both CSV and JSON, is available at etsy.com/your/account/privacy under Download Data. It takes longer — you request it, wait for an email, confirm, then come back and enter your password to download. Only worth doing if the files above turn out to be missing something.

Please don't share your Etsy password with anyone. Everything needed is in the files above.
